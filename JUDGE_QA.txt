WHYX — quick judge answers

Q: How is the 78% confidence calculated?
A: It is not a claimed probability. In this PoC it is a deterministic evidence score computed from observed revenue movement, inventory deterioration and stockout deterioration. The exact formula is exposed in the app under “Confidence methodology” and documented in the README. Production would replace it with calibrated statistical/causal confidence.

Q: Why do you call marketing a connected source?
A: marketing.csv is actively used as a corroborating market-pressure proxy. For West India, the app computes week-over-week conversion movement and feeds that signal into the competitor/market-pressure hypothesis. We explicitly label it as a proxy rather than direct competitor-price evidence.

Q: Why is the LLM not calculating the answer?
A: Quantitative truth must be reproducible and auditable. KPI computation, anomaly detection, evidence scoring, thresholding and recommendation eligibility stay deterministic. An LLM can sit on top for intent understanding and narrative generation.

Q: What happens when the system is uncertain?
A: It abstains. If no hypothesis clears the 65% action threshold, WHYX does not recommend an operational intervention and instead proposes the next-best investigation.

Q: Is the confidence calibrated?
A: Not yet. These are transparent PoC evidence scores, not calibrated probabilities. Calibration, backtesting and causal validation are explicit production requirements.
